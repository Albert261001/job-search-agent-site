Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Job Search Agent is a personal desktop application used by a single individual. It runs locally on Windows and is not offered as a public service.

Google Account Access

The application requests read-only Gmail access using the gmail.readonly permission to identify and process job alert emails.

It does not send, delete, or modify emails.

Information Processing

The application processes relevant email content, including job descriptions, senders, subjects, job links, and related metadata, to identify and organize employment opportunities.

Data Storage and Sharing

Job information, processing records, and selected source email content may be stored locally on the user's computer.

Structured job information is synchronized with the user's private Notion workspace for personal job application management.

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for personal job-search management and recovery until the user removes it from the local application or Notion workspace.

Access Revocation

Google Account access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account connections page . Revoking access does not automatically delete previously stored local or Notion data.

Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or data handling.
